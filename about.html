Our Story & Our Commitment

Serving the Bay Islands with integrity, expertise, and a local touch since 1991.

Providing Security for Your Island Life

Arlie Thompson Insurance has been the cornerstone of protection for the Bay Islands for over three decades. From our humble beginnings in 1991, we have built a reputation based on trust, reliability, and an unwavering commitment to the communities of Roatan, Utila, and Guanaja.

The Leadership

Today, the brokerage is proudly led by brothers Brennan and Devon Thompson. Building upon the strong foundation set by our founder, they lead a "dream team" dedicated to personalized service. We believe in being more than just an insurance provider; we are your neighbors, your partners, and your advocates.

Meet the Team

The strength of Arlie Thompson Insurance lies in our people. Alongside Brennan and Devon, Kerwin Borden and Deborah Webster provide the expert support and dedicated service our clients have come to rely on. Together, we work tirelessly to ensure that you, your family, your property, and your business remain protected.
