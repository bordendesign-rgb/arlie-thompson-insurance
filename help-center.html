Help Center

Auto & Boat Accidents

Your safety is our priority. Follow these steps immediately following an incident:

  • No Third Party: Call Arlie Thompson Insurance immediately. We will arrive to assess damage, document the scene with photos, and record your statement. Afterward, visit your trusted car shop for repair quotes, then file your claim online, where you can also upload your own photos along with your quotes and/or invoices.
  • Third Party Involved: Call the police immediately. You may also contact us, and we will coordinate police arrival for you. Follow the same documentation and repair quote process as above, including the ability to upload your own photos, quotes, and invoices through our online claims portal.

Home & Business Claims

For property-related incidents, please follow this protocol:

  • Secure the property and ensure everyone is safe.
  • Obtain repair quotes from your chosen contractors.
  • Prepare a brief statement of the incident.
  • We will either schedule an onsite visit to photograph and assess the damage, or you may upload photos and documentation directly through our online claims portal.
